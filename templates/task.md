## Goal

What should be true when this is finished, and why it matters. Two or three sentences.
Describe the outcome, not the implementation — the workflow decides how.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [test] An observable criterion someone else could check — tag: [test] | [browser] | [review]
- [ ] [browser] Another one
- [ ] [test] Edge case or failure behaviour that must hold

## Notes

Constraints, links, prior decisions, anything that would otherwise be guessed.
Say explicitly if something is out of scope.
